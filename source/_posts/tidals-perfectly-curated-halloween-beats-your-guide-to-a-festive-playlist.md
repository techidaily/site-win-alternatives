DEX 3, PCDJ’s [top DJ software](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/), is equipped with streaming support for TIDAL, providing DJs with in-app access to over 90 million songs and 450,000 videos.

One of the bigger benefits for subscribers is access to curated playlists that cover all genres, events, and holidays. If you’re looking for that last-minute Halloween Party Playlist you can have your pick with TIDAL. Just search TIDAL’s website for “Halloween” and click on “Playlists” to view a vast selection of curated playlists. Find something you like and click the “Add” (heart), launch DEX 3 and the playlist will appear beneath the TIDAL tree in the browser.

TIDAL’s team has provided many of the available playlists but if you ain’t scared – bang “Add” next to “Alice Cooper: The Halloween Playlist”. That’s right, a curated playlist from the bat biter himself!
