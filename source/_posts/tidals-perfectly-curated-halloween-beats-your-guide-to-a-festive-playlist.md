One of the bigger benefits for subscribers is access to curated playlists that cover all genres, events, and holidays. If you’re looking for that last-minute Halloween Party Playlist you can have your pick with TIDAL. Find something you like and click the “Add” (heart), launch DEX 3 and the playlist will appear beneath the TIDAL tree in the browser.
