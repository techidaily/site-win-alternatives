## Exploring the New Enhancements of PCDJ's Digital Video Playground

[![](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/thevideopool-newfeatures-coverimage.jpg?resize=530%2C298&ssl=1)](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/thevideopool-newfeatures-coverimage.jpg?fit=530%2C298&ssl=1 "thevideopool-newfeatures-coverimage")

**The Video Pool**, a [music video subscription service](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) for professional DJs and VJs, has always been on the market’s leading-edge — showcasing a modern web portal and producing many custom and exclusive video remixes.

Video mixing **DJs and VJs using DEX 3** have found a home with The Video Pool service, as DEX 3 will play the **supplied HD videos** at full-resolution and allow users to **[record a video](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/)** mix set in full-HD.

Today The Video Pool announced a few new changes users of the service will welcome.

### New Features Added To The Video Pool August 2015

![](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/videopool-dt-1.png?fit=100%2C100&ssl=1 "videopool-dt-1")

  
#### **Download Twice**

#### 

Now you won’t lose a credit when you download a video twice. This helps for partial downloads and lost connections or if you forgot your download history.

![](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/videopool-tc-2.png?fit=100%2C100&ssl=1 "videopool-tc-2")

  
#### Trending Category

They’ve listened and added a monthly trending category for video mixing DJs and VJs to quickly see what the hottest videos we’ve added for the month. Videos are ranked by most downloaded for the month.

![](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/videopool-ra-3.png?fit=100%2C100&ssl=1 "videopool-ra-3")

  
#### **Restricted Access**

Subscribers now have their own user member center with custom user profile, including bio. Log in to your restricted area to view download history and available credits.
